GET TO KNOW RUNETERRA
Runeterra is a planet brimming with life, magic, and diverse civilizations across multiple continents and archipelagos. The world is composed of a material realm and a mirrored spirit realm, and its history is marked by cataclysmic events like the ancient Rune Wars, which nearly destroyed the world with powerful magic.
